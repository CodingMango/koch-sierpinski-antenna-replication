# Verification outputs

The images in this folder show figures from Azzouz et al., "Characterization and Analysis of Hybrid Fractal Antennas for Multiband Communication and Radar Applications", Fractal Fract. 2026, 10, 47 (doi:10.3390/fractalfract10010047), with our drawings added on top. The article is published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); copyright of the original figures remains with its authors.

Changes made: lines or points were drawn over the figures (the geometry regenerated by `geometry/make_geometry.py`, or the digitized curve points). The Fig 1 image used by `scripts/00c_geometry_overlay_check.py` is an upscaled copy of the paper's figure. The images were cropped or downscaled for display.
